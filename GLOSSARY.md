# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around buying courses and getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
Someone who takes courses on Cadence.
_Avoid_: Student, User

**Instructor**:
A member of Cadence who owns and builds courses.
_Avoid_: Teacher, Author, Creator

**Platform Admin**:
A member of Cadence staff who runs the platform. Only a Platform Admin decides Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), the team, Staff

**Team Admin**:
A Learner who buys and manages a Team Licence.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own)

**Lapsed Learner**:
A Learner who has progress on a course but no Enrollment in it.
_Avoid_: Inactive, Expired

### Purchases

**Enrollment**:
A Learner's place on a course. A Learner has at most one per course, whether it came from an Individual Purchase or a Claimed Seat.
_Avoid_: Place on a course, Access

**Purchase**:
A single payment for a course. It is either an Individual Purchase or a Team Licence.
_Avoid_: Order, Transaction

**Individual Purchase**:
A Purchase a Learner makes for themselves. It gives the buyer an Enrollment.

**Team Licence**:
A Purchase a Team Admin makes for others. It grants Seats, not an Enrollment for the buyer.
_Avoid_: Team purchase, Bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One place on a Team Licence that a Learner can claim to get an Enrollment.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Code, Voucher

**Unclaimed Seat**:
A Seat no Learner has claimed yet.

**Claimed Seat**:
A Seat a Learner has claimed. It belongs to that Learner.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
The buyer's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for one specific Purchase. A Platform Admin approves or declines it. One Purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Platform Admin has decided yet. The buyer keeps their Enrollment while it is open, and a Team Licence's Unclaimed Seats can't be claimed.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has accepted. Approving it ends what the Purchase granted and leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has turned down. Nothing the Purchase granted is lost, it has no Refund, and the buyer may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The full amount paid, owed back to the buyer once their Refund Request is approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the buyer.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.

**Watched**:
Seconds of video a Learner has actually played in a course: the furthest point reached in each lesson, added up. Not lessons opened.
_Avoid_: Viewed, Progress

**Watch Limit**:
The most a buyer can have Watched of a course and still raise a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Watch cap, Viewing threshold
